## Add one to TradingView in 2 minutes (free plan works)

1. Sign in at [tradingview.com](https://www.tradingview.com). A free account is enough.
2. Open a chart, for example **NIFTY**.
3. On GitHub, open the indicator file above and click the **Copy raw file** button (two overlapping squares, top-right of the code).
4. In TradingView, click **Pine Editor** at the bottom of the screen.
5. Select everything in the editor, delete it, and paste.
6. Click **Save**, give it a name, then click **Add to chart**.

Full guide with settings: [docs/how-to-add-to-tradingview.md](docs/how-to-add-to-tradingview.md)

The free plan allows 2 indicators per chart, so you can use both indicators together.
